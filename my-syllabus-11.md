# My syllabus 11

Text reference for session 2026-27, transcribed on 14 Nov 2026 from the school's syllabus planning document. It covers only the school-exam subjects used in Study Buddy: maths, economics, political science, sociology and the English papers. The free-thinking subjects (history, psychology, philosophy) are not school-exam subjects and are left out on purpose.

Use this as the source of truth for chapter scope in Study Buddy and the Exam Cracker.

2. Inequalities
3. Angles & Arc length
4. Trigonometric function
5. Compound & multiple angles
6. Permutation & Combination
7. Binomial theorem
8. Set
9. Relation & functions

Monday test (Term II): Complex Number, Permutation & Combination, Binomial theorem, Sequence and series.

## Economics

**Term I:** Ch 1 Definition of economics; Ch 2 Basic concepts of economics; Ch 3 Basic problems of an economy; Ch 4 Types of economies; Ch 5 Solutions to the basic economic problems under different economic systems; Ch 6 Economic growth and development; Ch 7 Parameters (indicators) of development; Ch 8 Sustainable development; Ch 17 Definition, scope, importance and limitations of statistics; Ch 18 Collection, organization and presentation of data; Ch 19 Measures of central value; Ch 20 Measures of dispersion.

Monday test (Term I): Ch 1, Ch 2, Ch 19 (few topics).
Project 1: Background of four fathers of Economics.

**Term II:** Ch 9 Planning & Economic Development in India; Ch 10 Indian Economy Post Liberalization; Ch 11 Structural Changes in the Indian Economy After Liberalization; Ch 12 The Problem of Poverty in India; Ch 13 Profile of Indian Agriculture/Rural Development; Ch 14 Human Capital Formation in India; Ch 15 Unemployment in India – Problems and Policies; Ch 20 Measures of Dispersion (continued); Ch 21 Correlation; Ch 22 Index Number; Ch 23 Some Mathematical Tools Used in Economics.

Monday test (Term II): Ch 11, Ch 20 continued (few topics).
Project 2: based on measures of central value.

## Political Science

**Term I**
- Section A: 1 Introduction to Political Science; 2 Fundamental Concepts; 3 Political Ideologies; 4 Sovereignty.
- Section B: 5 International Relations: Disintegration of Soviet Union; 6 Regional cooperation: ASEAN and SAARC; 7 US Unilateralism: case studies.

Monday test (Term I): Introduction to Political Science, Fundamental Concepts.
Project (Term I, PPT): Right to Freedom and Role of Mass Media / US dominance in world politics in the post Cold War era / Crisis in the Middle East and its impact on World Order.

**Term II**
- Section A: Law; Liberty; Equality; Justice.
- Section B: US Unilateralism; Indo-US relation post 1991; Regional Cooperation: European Union, BRICS, QUAD; India's Relation with her Neighbours.

Monday test (Term II): Law, Liberty, European Union.
Project (Term II): Rise of Populism Across Democracies / Feminist Foreign Policy / Global Refugee Crisis / Pressure Groups and Lobbying in India.
